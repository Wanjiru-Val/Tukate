# Tukate — Brand decisions (working)

_Last updated: 11 Nov 2026_

## Core
- **App name:** Tukate (Sheng: "let's go" / hints at "kukata maji", let's drink)
- **Slogan:** Ingia Tukate
- **Tagline:** Swipe. Match. Cheers.
- **Concept:** Tinder-style swiping to find someone nearby to drink and hang out with. Friends, not dates. Safe by design (18+, meet in public, share live location).
- **Group feature (later):** Mbogi Mode
- **Buttons:** English for now (Get started, Pass, Super Like, Send message, Keep swiping)
- **Swahili/Sheng kept for headlines and flavour:** "Form imeshika!" (match screen), "Kinywaji", "Base"

## Visual direction: Bendera Remix (in progress)
Inspired by the Kenyan flag colours, turned up, with bright pops. Not a copy of the flag.

## Logo direction: classic red party cup + location pin
Reference chosen: the classic red party cup (red body, white rim, horizontal ridges). Cup red #FF3B3B, ridges #C92424, white rim.

Party cup concepts on the canvas:
1. Party cup in a pin: red cup inside a green pin
2. Cup as the map marker: the cup is the pin, with a green ring on the ground
3. Two cups, cheers: two red cups clinking, small green pin above
4. Bendera band cup: red cup, white rim, green base with a thin black line and a printed white pin

Earlier pin concepts (superseded by the red cup reference): pin + green cup, pin + shots, literal flag-stripe cup (trademark risk), remix-stripe cup pin.
